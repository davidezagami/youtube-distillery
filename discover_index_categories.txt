Identify between 6 and 10 practical categories that would help decide which videos are worth transcribing.
- Use categories that separate likely useful videos from likely irrelevant videos.
- Prefer concrete content themes over vague labels.
- Avoid one category containing almost all videos.
- Include both on-topic and off-topic buckets when the channel warrants it.

Output each category on its own line, prefixed with "- ". Nothing else.

Example output:
- Sales, Closing, and Persuasion
- Customer Acquisition and Lead Generation
- Founder Mindset and Personal Development
